E-commerce Sales Data Quality and Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data from 2021 to 2025 to evaluate revenue performance, identify leading markets and products, investigate data-quality issues, and translate findings into actionable business recommendations.

The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel, combining data preparation, exploratory analysis, PivotTables, KPI calculations, and an interactive dashboard.

Tools: Microsoft Excel  
Skills: Data cleaning, data quality assessment, exploratory data analysis, business intelligence, dashboard development, and data storytelling.

Business Questions

This project investigates six questions:

1. How did completed revenue change between 2021 and 2025?
2. Which countries contributed the most to completed revenue?
3. Which product categories and individual products generated the highest revenue?
4. What patterns appear in monthly revenue?
5. Which data-quality issues could affect the reliability of the findings?
6. What business actions should be considered based on the results?

Key Findings

1. Revenue growth

Completed revenue increased from $715,705 in 2021 to $1,387,346 in 2025, representing approximately 93.8% growth.

Business implication: The increase warrants further investigation into order volume, average order value, product mix, and market contribution.

2. Leading markets

The United States generated $1,486,369 in completed revenue, followed by China at $1,019,403.

Business implication: Further market-level analysis could identify the products and customer segments contributing to these results.

3. Nigeria's revenue growth

Completed revenue in Nigeria increased from $39,865 in 2021 to $173,878 in 2025.

Business implication: This growth makes Nigeria a useful candidate for further market analysis, including demand, product mix, and profitability.

4. Leading product

Redmi Note 12 generated $378,364 in completed revenue, making it the highest-revenue product identified in the analysis.

Business implication: Product availability, demand consistency, and profitability should be examined before making inventory or promotional decisions.

 5. November revenue pattern

Combined November completed revenue across the years was $789,099.

Business implication: Comparing November across individual years would help establish whether this pattern reflects recurring seasonality or specific sales events.

 Data Quality Assessment

The project includes a structured review of missing values and potential inconsistencies.

A significant issue was missing customer names: 9,744 records, approximately 99.25% of the dataset reviewed, had blank customer-name values.

Rather than inventing missing information, the analysis documented the issue and retained unresolved values for further investigation.

Supporting documentation includes a data dictionary, missing-values audit, and data-quality log.

Methodology

The workflow covered:

1. Dataset inspection and understanding.
2. Data-quality assessment.
3. Data preparation and documentation.
4. Exploratory analysis using Excel PivotTables.
5. KPI calculation and validation.
6. Dashboard development.
7. Interpretation of findings and formulation of business recommendations.

Limitations

- Missing customer names limit customer-level analysis.
- Some data-quality issues require further verification.
- Product costs and operating expenses were unavailable, so profitability could not be established.
- Observed trends do not establish causation.

Revenue should not be interpreted as profit, and business recommendations should be validated against additional operational and financial information.

Business Recommendations

- Investigate the drivers of annual revenue growth.
- Examine performance in major markets and assess opportunities for sustainable growth.
- Investigate Nigeria's increasing revenue contribution.
- Review leading products alongside availability and profitability data.
- Compare monthly results across individual years to assess seasonality.
- Improve data collection and validation to support more reliable reporting.
